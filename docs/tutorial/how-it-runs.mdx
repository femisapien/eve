The first exchange with your analytics assistant starts a session and runs a turn. eve checkpoints its work in steps:

| Term        | Meaning                                           |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **session** | Your whole conversation (durable, can span days). |
| **turn**    | One message you send and the work it triggers.    |
| **step**    | A durable checkpoint within the turn.             |

eve saves progress at step boundaries. Completed steps replay their recorded result, while a step interrupted mid-execution runs again. Give non-idempotent side effects such as charges and emails an idempotency key. A turn parked for approval or a question resumes when you answer.

The [metric glossary](./remember-definitions) uses durable state across turns; [spend approval](./guard-the-spend) parks until you answer. Continue with [querying sample data](./query-sample-data), or read the full [execution model](../concepts/execution-model-and-durability).
