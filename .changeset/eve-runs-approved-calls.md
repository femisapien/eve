eve now runs approved tool calls itself: each approved call re-checks its approval policy, streams `action.partial` and `action.result`, and runs with the tools of the step that asked. A call waiting for approval stays out of session history until every call of its step has a result, and the model is not called while any approval from that step is open. Session history no longer holds the AI SDK's `tool-approval-request` and `tool-approval-response` parts, so code that read approvals from history must read `input.requested` and `input.resolved` events instead. eve no longer adds a `[Pending approvals]` note to history. A typed reply such as "approve" answers the approvals it matches, except those a response policy decides, and the rest keep the turn waiting; it answers a relayed question when that question is the only one waiting. Answers to requests the step doesn't hold wait for the next step instead of being dropped, a policy's automatic denial reports `rejected` like a person's, and cancelling a turn reports each open approval in its own `input.resolved` event.
